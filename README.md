## 🌍 How Server Location Affects Your iGaming Platform
 
Server location decides how far your data must travel to reach every player. These are the four areas where distance makes the biggest difference.
 
### ⚡ Latency and Live Betting
 
Latency is the time a request takes to travel from the player to your server and back. In live betting, odds can change in a second or two, so a slow connection can mean a **missed bet or a rejected slip**.
 
Data cannot beat physics, and a server on the other side of the world will always be slower than one close to your players.
 
### 🚀 Page Speed and Player Experience
 
Players leave slow casinos in seconds. A server close to your audience loads lobbies, game thumbnails and cashier pages faster, which keeps players engaged and helps conversions.
 
Speed also supports your SEO, because search engines favor fast websites.
 
### 🎥 Live Dealer Streaming
 
Live dealer games stream video in real time. Poor routes between your server and the player cause buffering, quality drops and out of sync tables.
 
A well-connected location with enough bandwidth, such as a 10 Gbps port, keeps streams smooth even when many tables are busy.
 
### 🔌 Provider, Odds Feed and Payment Connections
 
Your platform never works alone. It talks all day to game providers, odds feeds, payment gateways and KYC services.
 
If those partners are hosted in Europe, a server in a well-connected European hub shortens every round trip, so bets, deposits and withdrawals process faster.
 
> [!TIP]
> **Rule of thumb:** the shorter the distance between player, server and partners, the faster every bet, deposit and payout.

## 🛑 Server Location and DDoS Attacks
 
DDoS attacks are one of the biggest threats to iGaming sites, and location plays a bigger part than most owners expect. Let us see why you are a target and how these attacks work.
 
> 🌊 A **DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service)** attack floods your network or server with fake traffic from thousands of hacked devices. Real players cannot get through, and your casino or sportsbook slows down or goes offline.
 
### 🎯 Why iGaming Sites Are Targeted
 
Attackers know a casino loses money every minute it is down. Some demand payment to stop the attack, while others are rivals trying to push players away during a final or a big promotion.
 
### 🧨 Common Types of DDoS Attacks
 
| ⚔️ Attack type | 🔍 What it does |
|---|---|
| 🌊 **Volumetric** | Floods your connection with so much data that the pipe fills up. |
| 🧱 **Protocol** | Abuses network protocols, such as SYN floods, to exhaust servers and firewalls. |
| 🎭 **Application layer** | Sends requests that look like real players, hitting login, search or cashier pages again and again. |
 
### 🗺️ How Location Helps
 
A well-connected data center usually has more upstream capacity, better peering and more routes for traffic to reach you. A hub like Amsterdam can absorb more traffic before it becomes a problem than a small facility with limited links.
 
Hardware-level filtering at the network edge can then drop common attack traffic before it reaches your server.
 
> [!CAUTION]
> Location alone will not stop an attack. **You need layers.**

## 🔒 How to Protect Your iGaming Site From DDoS Attacks
 
Strong protection is built in layers, not bought as one product. Follow these steps to make your casino or betting platform much harder to take offline.
 
### 🧅 The Layered Defense at a Glance
 
```text
 [ Players ]
      │
      ▼
 [ CDN / WAF / Reverse proxy ]   filters bad requests, hides your origin IP
      │
      ▼
 [ Firewall + Rate limiting ]    blocks unused ports, caps abusive traffic
      │
      ▼
 [ iGaming Dedicated Server ]    game logic, wallet, application layer
      │
      ▼
 [ Private Database ]            never open to the internet
```
 
### ✅ 9 Steps to Protect Your Platform
 
| # | 🛡️ Step | 📝 What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1️⃣ | **Pick a host with DDoS protection included** | Ask if it is part of the price or a paid add-on that starts after the first attack. |
| 2️⃣ | **Put a CDN, WAF or reverse proxy in front of your web layer** | It absorbs application layer attacks and filters bad requests before they reach your origin server. |
| 3️⃣ | **Hide your origin IP** | If attackers find it, they can bypass your CDN. Keep it private and accept web traffic only from your protection layer. |
| 4️⃣ | **Close what you do not need** | Block unused ports with a firewall and keep your database port closed to the internet, so only your application can reach it. |
| 5️⃣ | **Use rate limiting and bot filtering** | Limit login, registration and cashier requests per IP so fake traffic cannot flood sensitive pages. |
| 6️⃣ | **Separate critical systems** | On larger platforms, run the wallet, database and game servers on different machines, so trouble in one does not freeze everything. |
| 7️⃣ | **Plan for headroom** | Choose a server with spare CPU, RAM and bandwidth, so a spike does not take you down. A 10 Gbps port gives far more room than 1 Gbps. |
| 8️⃣ | **Monitor around the clock** | Track traffic, CPU, response times and disk space, and set alerts so you notice trouble in minutes. |
| 9️⃣ | **Prepare an incident plan** | Know who to call at your host, how to reroute traffic and how to update players. Practice it before you need it. |
 
> [!WARNING]
> **Be realistic.** Basic protection handles common attacks well, but very large or highly targeted attacks may need extra layers, such as a dedicated scrubbing service. Ask your host exactly what is included and what you can add.

## 🧭 How to Choose the Right Server Location for Your iGaming Site
 
There is no single best location for every platform. The right choice depends on where your players, partners and regulators are. Tick through this list before you decide:
 
- [ ] 👥 **Where are most of my players?** Place servers as close to your biggest audience as possible.
- [ ] 🔌 **Where are my providers hosted?** Game providers, odds feeds and payment gateways should be a short hop away.
- [ ] 📜 **What do my license conditions say?** Check rules on data and system location.
- [ ] 📡 **How strong is the network?** Look at peering, internet exchanges, uplink speeds and data center tier.
- [ ] ✍️ **Is gambling allowed in writing?** Read the acceptable use policy, because a host that bans gambling can shut you down without warning.
- [ ] 📈 **Can I grow here?** Check for bigger servers, custom builds and migration help later.
